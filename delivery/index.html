Delivery and payment

Delivery in Lahore

We deliver through inDrive. The fare depends on distance and is not included in the price. Pickup from our shop is free.

Order 4 hours ahead

We need 4 hours to prepare, counted between 9:00 and 22:00 Lahore time. Choose your time at checkout.

Pay by transfer

How much is delivery?

Delivery is done through inDrive, so the fare depends on distance. It is paid separately and is not included in product prices.

Yes. Add it on the product page or in the cart for each item.
